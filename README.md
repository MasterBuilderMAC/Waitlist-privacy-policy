Privacy Policy for Waitlist Estimator
Last updated: October 16, 2026

Waitlist Estimator is a Safari extension that reads the guest list on Tabit pages you are already viewing in your own account and displays an estimated wait time.

Data collection: The app does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. All processing happens on your device, in your browser.

Local storage: The extension may save settings (such as calculation preferences) on your device using Safari’s local extension storage. This data never leaves your device.

Third-party content: The extension reads information displayed on Tabit’s website to calculate estimates. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tabit.
